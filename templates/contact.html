QGene Research Project

QGene is an academic research project exploring hybrid classical-quantum machine learning for genomic variant classification. We welcome collaboration with researchers, clinicians, and institutions working in precision medicine and computational genomics.

Department of Computer Science
Pune, Maharashtra, India
github.com/QGene

QGene is an academic prototype. It is not a commercial product and should not be used for clinical diagnosis. All predictions are for research purposes only.
