How it works

Three steps to variant classification

QGene runs your variant through four models simultaneously — two classical, one quantum, one hybrid — and returns a single explainable result.

1

Enter variant details

Input the gene (BRCA1/BRCA2), genomic position, mutation type, and amino acid change. No account required.

2

Hybrid analysis runs

Random Forest and SVM provide classical predictions. QSVM uses a ZZFeatureMap quantum kernel for a fundamentally different geometric representation. All four outputs are weighted into a hybrid ensemble.

3

Download your report

Get a PATHOGENIC or BENIGN classification with confidence score, per-model breakdown, SHAP feature importance, and a downloadable PDF report.
