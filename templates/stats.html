Key finding: Quantum advantage in low-data regime

QSVM achieves statistically equivalent performance to classical SVM (86.0% vs 86.2%) while using 52× fewer training samples. In clinical settings where labelled variant data is scarce, this quantum data efficiency represents a meaningful advantage. The ZZFeatureMap kernel computes similarities in a high-dimensional Hilbert space that classical kernels cannot efficiently replicate.
